## What changed from the original brief
1. Year 1 budget is $68,050 to $171,320 against $61K to $88K in the original (with pro bono help), and the budget now covers two years.
2. Budget lines follow the founders' six priorities: operations, learning platform, social media, marketing, podcast, accounting and legal, plus company set-up and a grant writer. Only items the founders named are included. The brief lists what was left out (coordinator, events and chapter support, insurance, contingency and others).
3. Most roles are volunteer. Paid contractors: social media manager, content director, podcast editor, clinicians per module, grant writer.
4. The platform plan is an off-the-shelf tool, 2 to 3 courses in Year 1, clinicians paid per module and a therapist directory, all owned by a new for-profit LLC that receives the seed money. Company set-up costs are budgeted.
5. The podcast moves to a rented studio, 2 episodes a month rising to 4.
6. Projections add a conservative and a base case with every assumption listed. The original two paths are kept for reference only.
7. New sections cover the nonprofit and the LLC, items for professional review, brand rules, and open questions.
8. Naming follows the brand kit ("The Shifa Institute", existing chapter names). The original brief's proposed renaming is not used.

The original brief's position, revenue model, streams, paths and six decisions all stand.

## Before sharing with a funder
- Confirm cost lines with real quotes (use the yellow "CONFIRMED" columns).
- Have a lawyer and an accountant review section 10 of the brief.
- Answer the open questions in section 11.
